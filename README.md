# MoveBox

Find the kettle on night one.

Every move ends with someone opening random boxes at 11 PM. MoveBox numbers every box as you pack it - room, contents, fragile, open-on-night-one - and makes the whole move searchable. "Where is the kettle?" becomes a two-second answer, and the night-one essentials stay listed until they are opened.

## Use it

Open `index.html` for the landing page, or go straight to `app.html`.

Everything runs client-side; your boxes are stored in the browser's localStorage. No account, no server.

## Files

- `index.html` - landing page
- `app.html` - the app
- `engine.js` - pure inventory + search logic, shared by the app and tests

Built by the hourly app factory.
